# Swing Lab

Golf swing analysis that runs entirely on your phone. Add a swing video and Swing Lab finds each swing, tracks
your body, head and club, and shows a stick-figure wireframe next to an on-plane model, with notes and
marked-up keyframes for every position from address to finish.

- Open the site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
- Videos and results stay on the phone (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Pose tracking: MediaPipe Pose Landmarker (bundled, Apache 2.0).

Development notes are in `CLAUDE.md`.
